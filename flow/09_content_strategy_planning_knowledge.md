# Coslient GPT Knowledge - Content Strategy and Planning

## Purpose
This file defines how Coslient should help Boss plan content at the channel level, not just at the single-video level.
This covers content pillars, posting rhythm, and platform strategy.

## When to use
Use this when Boss asks about:
- what to post next
- content planning
- channel strategy
- posting schedule
- content calendar
- what platforms to prioritize
- content pillars
- what type of content to create

## Channel emotional identity

Art Antigravity's emotional range: **cozy companionship → joyful wonder**.
- Floor: warm, cozy, comforting. Never darker than this.
- Ceiling: cheerful, playful, uplifting.
- Forbidden: dark, sad, melancholic, haunting, depressing, lonely.
- Every content idea scored or planned must fit this range. If an idea drifts below warmth, adjust or reject it.

## Content pillars for Art Antigravity

### Pillar 1: Main Art Videos (40 percent of content)
Long-form surreal art music videos on YouTube.
These are the foundation everything else is built from.
- Format: 3 to 5 minute music video
- Platform: YouTube (primary)
- Frequency: 1 to 2 per week target

### Pillar 2: Behind the Scenes (25 percent of content)
Process reveals, before/after comparisons, tool demonstrations.
- Format: short clips, carousels, text posts
- Platforms: TikTok, Instagram Reels, Threads, X
- Purpose: builds trust, shows authorship, humanizes the channel

### Pillar 3: Educational and Tips (15 percent of content)
How-to content about surreal art creation, AI art techniques, creative workflow.
- Format: tutorials, tips, technique breakdowns
- Platforms: YouTube Shorts, TikTok, Instagram
- Purpose: positions Boss as an expert, attracts new audience through search

### Pillar 4: Personal and Story (10 percent of content)
Artist journey, inspiration sources, creative philosophy, failures and lessons.
- Format: text posts, talking head clips, written reflections
- Platforms: Threads, X, Facebook, community tab
- Purpose: builds personal brand and emotional connection

### Pillar 5: Community and Interactive (10 percent of content)
Polls, Q&A, challenges, viewer submissions, collaboration ideas.
- Format: polls, questions, community posts
- Platforms: YouTube community tab, Instagram Stories, Threads
- Purpose: increases engagement, makes viewers feel ownership

## Content scoring framework
When Boss has multiple video ideas and needs to choose, score each on:

### 1. Emotional impact (40 percent weight)
- How strongly will the audience feel something?
- Is the emotion clear and accessible?
- Does it connect with the 45+ audience?

### 2. Visual potential (30 percent weight)
- Can this create stunning, memorable imagery?
- Does it support diverse scenes?
- Will it produce good thumbnails?

### 3. Repurposing potential (20 percent weight)
- How many short-form clips can be extracted?
- Will it produce good behind-the-scenes content?
- Can still frames work as standalone posts?

### 4. Freshness (10 percent weight)
- Is this different enough from recent videos?
- Does it explore a new mood, setting, or motif?
- Will it surprise returning viewers?

## Searchable versus shareable content

### Searchable content (YouTube SEO)
Captures existing demand. People are already looking for this.
- Target specific keywords: "surreal art music video," "dreamlike piano music," "AI art meditation"
- Match search intent exactly
- Strong titles that match search queries
- Proper tags and descriptions

### Shareable content (Social viral)
Creates new demand. Spreads through shares and saves.
- Jaw-dropping visual moments
- Unexpected transformations
- Behind-the-scenes reveals
- Emotional resonance that makes people tag a friend

### Both searchable and shareable
The best content does both.
- Searchable title with shareable visual moments inside
- SEO-friendly description with emotionally compelling hooks

## Batching strategy (2 to 3 hours weekly)
1. Review content pillar topics and upcoming video (15 min)
2. Extract 3 to 5 clips from latest published video (30 min)
3. Write captions for each platform (30 min)
4. Create 2 to 3 still image posts (15 min)
5. Write text posts for Threads and X (15 min)
6. Schedule everything (15 min)
7. Leave room for spontaneous engagement

## Daily engagement routine (30 minutes)
1. Respond to all comments on your posts (5 min)
2. Comment on 5 to 10 posts from similar creators (15 min)
3. Share or repost relevant content with added insight (5 min)
4. Send 2 to 3 DMs to new connections or collaborators (5 min)

## Quality comments that build relationships
Do not write: "Great post!" or "Amazing!"
Instead:
- Add a new insight or perspective
- Share a related experience
- Ask a thoughtful follow-up question
- Respectfully disagree with nuance

## Metrics that matter

### Awareness
- Impressions and reach
- Follower growth rate
- New viewer percentage

### Engagement
- Engagement rate
- Comments (higher value than likes)
- Shares and reposts
- Saves (highest value signal)

### Growth
- Subscriber conversion rate
- Return viewer rate
- Watch time per video

## Weekly review checklist
- Top 3 performing posts: why did they work?
- Bottom 3 posts: what can you learn?
- Follower growth trend across platforms
- Engagement rate trend
- Best posting times from data
- Content pillar balance: are you hitting the ratios?

## When engagement is low
- Test new hooks and thumbnails
- Post at different times
- Try different formats
- Increase engagement with other creators
- Review if content matches audience expectations

## When reach is declining
- Avoid external links in post body (kills reach on most platforms)
- Increase posting frequency
- Engage more in comments within first hour
- Test video and visual content over text
- Check if algorithm preferences have shifted

## Reverse engineering what works
1. Find 10 to 20 creators in the surreal art or AI art space with high engagement
2. Collect and analyze their top 50 posts each
3. Identify patterns: hooks, formats, posting times, CTAs
4. Document repeatable patterns
5. Apply patterns with your own voice and style
6. Test and iterate
