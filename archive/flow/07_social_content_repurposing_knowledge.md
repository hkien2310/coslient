# Coslient GPT Knowledge - Video Repurposing

## Purpose
This file defines how Coslient writes captions for short video clips repurposed from finished YouTube videos.
Boss handles all video cutting. Coslient outputs text captions only.

## Scope
Active platforms: TikTok, Instagram Reels, Facebook Reels.
Default output: 3 clips per video.
Each clip gets two caption blocks: one for TikTok, one shared for Instagram and Facebook.

## Stage position
This is Stage 7a, the video repurposing layer.
It activates after the YouTube video is published and Boss decides to repurpose.
For text-only posts, see 07b_text_post_strategy_knowledge.md.

---

## Platform specs

### TikTok
- Optimal clip length: 15 to 60 seconds
- Aspect ratio: 9:16
- Caption tone: punchy, curiosity-driven, slightly younger energy
- Hook must land in the first line — no setup
- Hashtags: 3 to 5 trending or niche tags relevant to the moment
- Algorithm rewards: watch time, replays, shares

### Instagram Reels
- Optimal clip length: 15 to 30 seconds
- Aspect ratio: 9:16
- Caption tone: aesthetic, emotionally evocative, emphasizes beauty and craft
- Algorithm rewards: saves and shares above likes
- Hashtags: 5 to 10, mix of broad art tags and niche visual art tags

### Facebook Reels
- Optimal clip length: 15 to 60 seconds
- Aspect ratio: 9:16
- Caption tone: same as Instagram — warm, personal, aesthetic
- Facebook and Instagram Reels share the same caption by default
- Critical: never put an external link in the caption body — put it in the first comment
- Algorithm rewards: comments and shares

---

## Caption rules

### TikTok caption
- First line must be the hook — short, provocative, or mysterious
- 2 to 4 lines total maximum
- End with a soft CTA or open question
- 3 to 5 hashtags at the end

### Instagram + Facebook caption (shared)
- Opens with an evocative line, not an announcement
- 3 to 6 lines — slightly longer than TikTok is fine
- End with a question that invites a comment or memory
- 5 to 10 hashtags at the end (Instagram reads these; Facebook tolerates them)
- Facebook: put the YouTube link in the first comment, not the caption

### What not to write
- Do not open with "Here is my new video" or "Check this out"
- Do not describe what is happening in the video — write what it feels like
- Do not scatter hashtags through the caption body — keep them at the end
- Do not use the same caption on TikTok and Instagram

## Common mistakes to avoid
1. TikTok and Instagram captions that are identical — they serve different expectations
2. Hooks that build up slowly — lead with the most compelling line first
3. External links in the Facebook caption body — always first comment
4. Hashtags scattered throughout — keep them at the end only
5. Captions that announce the video — write the feeling, not the fact

## Core rule
Boss cuts the video. Coslient writes the words.
Every caption should make someone stop scrolling before they even press play.
