Sometimes, the most profound memories are the ones we had to leave behind...

Hello to all my friends who love music and a touch of nostalgia. Today, I’m sharing a new musical story with you called "The Water Remembers."

This song was inspired by the old, evacuated coastal villages—places where the ocean has slowly embraced the stone houses, the old chapels, and the quiet remnants of everyday life. I've always believed that the water doesn’t destroy; rather, it gently preserves and protects our history in the most peaceful and silent place on earth.

With a slow 6/8 waltz rhythm that moves like the breathing of the sea, carried by the gentle heartbeat of a bodhrán and the warmth of a cello, I hope this piece brings a moment of deep calm to your soul.

Do you have a memory of a hometown or a beloved place you once had to leave behind? I invite you to pour yourself a hot cup of tea, listen to the music, and share your story with us in the comments below. I am always here to read your memories.
