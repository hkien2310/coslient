DESCRIPTION:
Sometimes, the most profound memories are the ones we had to leave behind. I wrote "The Water Remembers" after hearing stories of the old evacuated towns in the far north of Scotland, places like St Kilda, where the ocean finally reclaimed the stone houses and chapels that once held generations of life. There is a deep, quiet ache in walking away from your home, but there is also a strange, ancient peace in knowing that the water holds onto everything we lose. This song is my way of honoring the elders who had to pack up their lives, and the quiet dignity of hometowns that now sleep beneath the silver tide. I wanted to create a space where the ocean isn't a destroyer, but a gentle preserver—a massive, silent guardian watching over the iron pots, the sunday shoes, and the names of the people who used to walk those hills. I imagine a majestic sea dragon, moving slowly through the submerged streets, keeping our history safe in the cold depths. It is a waltz of memory, played in a rolling 6/8 rhythm that mimics the rocking of a boat leaving the shore. I hope this piece brings you a sense of calm and a reminder that nothing is truly lost if the water remembers. So please, pour yourself a hot cup of tea, settle into a comfortable chair, and let the gentle rhythm carry you out to sea.
