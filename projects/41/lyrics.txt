The blue cup caught the morning...
and I was eight years old again

The blue cup caught the morning
on my windowsill today
One touch and I was barefoot
down that little gravel lane

Grandpa by the screen door
laughing soft and warm
“Slow down, boy, your boots ain’t tied”
then he opened up the barn

I thought it was just another day
with dust and chores and sun
But some days hold your whole life
before you know what’s begun

That blue cup by the window
brought the whole farm back
Grandpa in his old brown hat
sunlight on his back

I was too young to know it
too busy growing up
But the whole world felt warm
in that little blue cup

That blue cup by the window
still remembers love

for the mare beside the rail

warm as summer rain

like I was strong enough
And somehow all those little things
were how he gave me love
