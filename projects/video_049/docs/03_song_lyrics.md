# Until the Streetlights Came On
**Artist:** The Young Druid
**Channel:** Coslient
**Status:** FINAL — LOCKED

Americana folk-pop, sweeping hummable melody, anthemic singalong chorus, bittersweet major-minor chord shifts, swung eighth notes, Andante 86 BPM, rolling fingerpicked acoustic guitar, warm upright bass, light banjo accents, brushed snare groove, weathered close-mic'd male storyteller vocal, warm tape saturation, emotional key change

**Runtime:** 5:44

## Full Lyrics with Production Notes

**[Intro]**
*[marble rolls across hardwood, clinks against another marble, settles — acoustic guitar enters warm and bright]*

---

**[Verse 1]**
*[LOW register — intimate, descending arches, close-mic'd, no percussion — stays well below chorus ceiling]*

The marble in the drawer was cold and round,
blue-green glass eye from sixty-three or four.
I held it still. It almost made a sound
of spinning tops on Danny's driveway floor.

*[brief fingerpicked guitar fill, 2 bars — breathing room]*

The chalk dust still lives somewhere in my skin,
the kite string still runs tight against my thumb.
Those August afternoons refused to end,
and none of us asked when we'd be done.

---

**[Pre-Chorus]**
*[brushed snare enters softly — melody steps UP one note per line, each phrase higher than the last, building toward the leap]*

The afternoon stretched lazy, slow and sweet,
we chased the shadows down the dusty lane.
There wasn't anywhere we'd rather hide
than down that block on that long summer streeeet... *[Hold note — build tension]*

---

**[Chorus 1]**
*[MELODIC LEAP — chorus BURSTS upward from pre-chorus plateau, bright and wide open]*
*[4-line arc: each line ascends toward the hook — "BARE-foot" leaps up, "golden" rises, "tried" peaks, "ONNN" lands]*
*[peak on "CAME" /AY/ — sustain "ONNN" /AH/ — the widest, most open vowel, longest held note in the song]*

Barefoot on the asphalt, warm and wide,
those long days were golden, every one.
The kite could reach the clouds if we just tried,
We PLAYED un-TIL the streetlights CAME ONNN.
*(streetlights came on... streetlights CAME ONNN...)*

---

**[Verse 2]**
*[piano enters softly — same LOW register as Verse 1, brushed snare continues, slightly richer but still intimate]*

Mrs. Walker's washing on the line
sent soap and cotton drifting through the heat.
The spinning top could wobble on its spine,
we learned to read the elm trees, not the street.

*[brief piano fill, 2 bars — breathing room]*

A piece of chalk, a kite tail, length of rope,
we built whole kingdoms out of Thursday noon.
We didn't know the limits of our hope,
not yet, not that particular afternoon.

---

**[Pre-Chorus 2]**
*[strings swell — same ascending step pattern but harder, more urgent, almost at the chorus leap now]*

The evening orange started in the west,
we saw the shadows growing long and fast.
We could've quit, but no one would suggest
being the first to say it couldn't last... *[Hold note — build tension]*

---

**[Chorus 2]**
*[backing harmonies enter — fuller arrangement, melodic leap AGAIN — arrival feels earned a second time]*

Barefoot on the asphalt, warm and wide,
those long days were golden, every one.
The kite could reach the clouds if we just tried,
We PLAYED un-TIL the streetlights CAME ONNN.
The screen door banged behind the last one home,
*(behind the last one home...)*
the game still going somewhere in our heads.
We didn't know those evenings would go soooon,
we were too busy running straight ahead.
*(running straight aheeead...)*

---

**[Bridge]**
*[piano only — HIGH and SOFT, falsetto register — melodically unfamiliar territory, the song has never been here before]*
*[rubato — time loosens, the singer floats above the song]*
*[sigh]*

We never said goodbye to any of it,
the marble games, the kite string, Danny's yard.
You don't know it's the last day when you're in it,
you're too busy laughing, playing hard.

*[deep breath]*

But I am glad for every one of those days,
every last one... until the streetlights came on.
