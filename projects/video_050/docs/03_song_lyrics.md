# Video 050 — Song Development

STAGE: Song Development
STATUS: approved

MUSIC DIRECTION:
- Genre/Fusion: Pastoral chamber folk + warm country ballad
- Tempo: 72 BPM (Andante)
- Instruments: Fingerpicked acoustic guitar, upright bass, cello, accordion, soft brushed snare
- Vocal persona: Mature female alto storyteller
- Signature motif: Tiếng xẻng nhỏ cắm vào đất (subtle) ở đầu bài

EMOTIONAL DIRECTION:
- Arc: Từ tĩnh lặng, cô độc an bình → Truyền giao thế hệ → Bùng nổ nhẹ nhàng trong sự chấp nhận hữu hạn của đời người.

MELODY DIRECTION:
- Melodic contour: Verse descending. Chorus melodic leap ở từ "bloooom" và "seeeee".
- Hook type: Target cả 3 (Lyrical, Melodic, Rhythmic).
- Vowel placement: Từ quan trọng nhất kết thúc bằng nguyên âm mở (OO, EE, AY).

SONG TITLE:
"Dirt on Her Hands"

STYLE:
pastoral chamber folk, soaring memorable melody, rolling 6/8 waltz groove, driving rhythmic pulse, fingerpicked acoustic guitar, emotional cello, warm accordion, mature female alto storyteller vocal, intimate close-mic

## LYRICS

[Intro: fingerpicked acoustic guitar, faint sound of soil shifting]

[Verse 1: guitar and voice only, intimate, unhurried]
The house sleeps through the morning chill.
She steps outside without a sound.
No gloves to keep her fingers clean,
she kneels directly on the ground.
She opens up a wooden box,
to find the seeds she saved last year...

[Pre-Chorus 1: upright bass enters, light cello swell, ascending]
The winter frost is breaking up,
the air is sharp across her face.
This patch of yard expects her here,
a quiet corner in the space.

[Chorus 1: accordion enters, wide stereo, soaring]
With dirt on her hands, she buries the seed,
she plants for the bloooom she won't live to seeeee.
The ground doesn't ask her how long she will stay,
it takes what she gives... and it shows her the way.

[Verse 2: guitar, rim clicks, string pad — shifting perspective to granddaughter]
Her granddaughter watches from the door,
then walks across the morning lawn.
She cups her hands to catch the soil,
and copies how the lines are drawn.
They pat the surface firm and flat,
and wipe the mud upon their jeans.

[Pre-Chorus 2: more tense strings, building]
The little girl looks at the dirt,
and asks how long a root will take.
She points toward the rising sun,
and feels the wind begin to wake.

[Chorus 2: brushed snare full, backing vocals added]
With dirt on her hands, she buries the seed,
she plants for the bloooom she won't live to seeeee.
(won't live to see)
The ground doesn't ask her how long she will stay,
it takes what she gives... and it shows her the way.
(shows her the way)

[Bridge: stripped back, piano and cello only, near-spoken]
[spoken, soft]
She stands up slow... and wipes her brow.
[sigh]
"I won't be here to watch it climb."
She looks down at the little girl.
"You'll see it open up, in time."

[Pause - absolute silence for one beat]

[Final Chorus: full band, key change, emotional climax]
[soaring vocal]
With dirt on OUR hands, we bury the seed!
We plant for the bloooom we might not live to seeeee!
(we might not live to see)
The ground doesn't ask us how long we will stay,
it takes what we give... and it shows us the way.
(it only grows... it only grows)

[Outro: fade to fingerpicked guitar only]
she steps outside...
without a sound...
(mmm-hmm)
