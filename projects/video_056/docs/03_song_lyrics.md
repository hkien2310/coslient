SONG TITLE:
the sun-dust horse

STYLE:
Indie folk ballad, Acoustic guitar, upright bass, brushed drums, piano, and a male baritone vocal, The acoustic guitar plays a steady fingerstyle pattern in 4/4 time at 80 BPM in the key of G major, The upright bass provides melodic counterpoint with a warm woody tone, Drums feature a light shuffling snare played with brushes and a soft kick on beats 1 and 3, A clean piano enters with sparse high-register chords in the second verse, The male lead vocal is intimate and weathered, occasionally layering into three-part folk harmonies during the chorus, The arrangement is organic and dynamic, with instruments entering and exiting to build texture, A solo cello enters only at the bridge, providing deep melodic warmth as the emotional peak.
