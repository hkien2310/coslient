Dưới đây là tổng hợp lại trọn bộ Bước 3 (Song Development) với bản phối The Summer Glass (76 BPM) đã được Boss chốt cho video này:

1. MUSIC & EMOTIONAL DIRECTION
Genre & Tempo: Cinematic mid-tempo Americana folk, 76 BPM. Cuộn trào và mang tính điện ảnh.

Instrumentation: Acoustic guitar quạt chả đều tay (steady strumming), trống snare vuốt chổi cọ (brushed snare), bass trầm ấm.

Vocal Identity: Giọng nam trung (baritone) từng trải, hát dứt khoát.

Emotional Arc: Sự kìm nén, ngột ngạt ở hiện tại vỡ oà thành sự giải thoát rực rỡ và xoa dịu ở điệp khúc.

2. HOOK & STRUCTURE STRATEGY
Structure: Sử dụng Pre-Chorus dồn nhịp để làm bệ phóng đẩy thẳng cảm xúc lên đoạn Chorus bùng nổ. Bridge tạo khoảng lặng ngắn trước khi chốt hạ.

Hook Phrase: "Everything heavy belongs to the ground / And the sky is just holding the past." (Tạo độ nảy và điểm rơi nhịp điệu cực mạnh ngay chữ "ground" và "past").

3. SUNO PACKAGE (FINAL)
SONG TITLE:
The Summer Glass

STYLE:
Cinematic mid-tempo Americana folk, 76 BPM. Steady acoustic guitar strumming, brushed snare drum rolling rhythm, warm upright bass. Mature male baritone vocal, weathered but energetic and emotionally resonant. Starts close, builds steady tension in the pre-chorus. The chorus blooms wide with an uplifting, sweeping folk melody and warm female backing harmonies. Bridge strips back to just vocal and single guitar strum before exploding into a triumphant final chorus with tape-room reverb. Warm, cathartic, highly engaging, and memorable.

LYRICS:

[Intro]
(soft acoustic guitar picking, building into a steady strum)
I carried the heavy brass out to the porch...
and let the hot afternoon go.

[Verse 1]
The city was tired, the highway was loud,
just rushing the people below.
I set up the legs on the old wooden floor,
and wiped off the dust from the lens.
I waited a while for the streetlights to fade...
and for the night sky to begin.

[Pre-Chorus]
(building rhythm, brushed snare enters)
We get so used to the noise and the gray.
We let the beautiful things slip away.

[Chorus]
(sweeping and engaging, full harmony)
But everything heavy belongs to the ground!
And the sky is just holding the past.
I thought I had lost all the magic we knew,
in a world that was moving too fast.
But fifty long years just melted away...
(softly: when I looked through the summer glass.)

[Verse 2]
I closed my left eye and I focused the wheel,
and the silver came into the frame.
I was back in the yard with my dad by my side,
calling the planets by name.
The exact same design, shining down on the roof,
like a promise that time couldn't break.

[Pre-Chorus]
We get so used to the noise and the gray.
We let the beautiful things slip away.

[Chorus]
(sweeping and engaging, full harmony)
But everything heavy belongs to the ground!
And the sky is just holding the past.
I thought I had lost all the magic we knew,
in a world that was moving too fast.
But fifty long years just melted away...
(softly: when I looked through the summer glass.)

[Bridge]
(music drops, just vocal and single guitar strums)
You can’t stop the clock, and you can’t stop the years!
We grow up, and we worry, and we try!
(snare drum roll building up)
But all of that trouble just falls out of sight...
when you finally look up at the sky!

[Final Chorus]
(maximum energy, triumphant folk lift)
Yeah, everything heavy belongs to the ground!
And the sky is just holding the past.
I thought I had lost all the magic we knew,
in a world that was moving too fast!
But fifty long years just melted away...
they just melted away.
Yeah, I found you again...
in the summer glass.

[Outro]
(acoustic guitar slowing down)
Everything heavy belongs to the ground...
[hold the last word gently]
I looked through the glass.
