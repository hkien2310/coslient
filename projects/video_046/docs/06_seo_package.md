## DESCRIPTION:
Welcome to Oakridge. This quiet brick-and-stone town sits at the edge of the universe, where old memories slowly blend with stardust.

For many of us, home is not just a place made of wood and clay. It is the repository of every laugh, every tear, and every song we ever played. I wrote "The Stars Still Sing My Name" as a quiet tribute to our elders—to the parents, grandparents, and old musicians who poured their lives into creating beautiful things, only to feel sometimes left behind by a fast-paced, modern world. Oakridge represents that safe haven in our hearts. It is the place that raised us, the place that remembers our stories even when our own eyes grow dim and our hands begin to shake on the guitar strings. This song is my way of saying: your music matters. The love you gave, the simple quiet lives you built, and the tunes you left behind are never truly lost. Even if the very bricks of our old homes float up into the cosmic blue, the universe itself will keep singing your name. You are not forgotten.

In this retro-cosmic acoustic waltz, we follow an aging musician sitting on his familiar porch, strumming his weathered acoustic guitar as the heavy gravity of the everyday world gently softens. Step by step, the red clay bricks of his old cottage detach and lift softly into the luminous indigo night sky. Written letters, carrying words that were never sent, fold themselves into delicate paper flowers and drift along the quiet stellar winds. It is a peaceful, beautiful transformation. The visuals are crafted in a soft, handcrafted storybook miniature style—using smooth claymation-inspired textures, warm matte woods, and cozy amber lighting with lifted soft shadows to bring a sense of gentle comfort to your screen. 

If you are looking for a quiet moment of healing, a breath of nostalgic air, or simply a peaceful space to sit and reflect, we hope this video brings you comfort.
