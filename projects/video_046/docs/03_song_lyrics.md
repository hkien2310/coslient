## SONG TITLE:
**The Stars Still Sing My Name**

## STYLE:
Vintage cinematic space-folk crooner ballad, slow 72 BPM waltz-sway, heavy rhythmic bass drum, weathered acoustic guitar, soft upright bass, warm Mellotron swells, retro radio static sweep, deep cosmic hum. Weathered and raspy male baritone storyteller vocal, intimate, close-mic'd, with long cosmic delays and atmospheric reverb. Soaring dream-pop female alto harmonies entering in the chorus. Begins with radio frequency sweep and clock ticking, sparse whispered verses, builds into a grand majestic exploding cosmic chorus with soaring echoing strings, bridge breakdown, epic space-folk outro.
