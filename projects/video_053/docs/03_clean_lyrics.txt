[Intro, fingerpicked acoustic guitar, 76 BPM, audible fret noise and string buzz]

[Verse 1]
The calluses run deep along the palm,
forty years of stone and iron rail.
Tonight I sat down with a borrowed guitar
and pressed my thumb against the scale.
The old record spins her favorite song,
the one she loved when we were young.
I pressed the first three strings until they bled
and played the whole night long.

[Pre-Chorus 1]
[upright bass enters quietly]
The needle finds the groove and holds its breath.
(holds its breath)
I press the chord. I press again.
(press again)

[Chorus 1]
[cello enters — emotional signature of the song]
Heavy strings, heavy hands,
heavy years and what I knew.
(what I knew)
The chord I keep on missing
is the word I couldn't say to you.
(couldn't say to you)
I'll press until I finally make it through,
(make it through)
I'll press until it's TRUUUE.
(until it's true)

[Verse 2]
[soft brushed snare enters, guitar tone slightly warmer]
The third step from the kitchen has a groan —
I've heard it every night since you were gone.
(since you were gone)
The paper chords I wrote are barely there,
worn down from all the times I got them wrong.
Last Monday night I played the whole thing through,
and got it right. The melody ran plain.
And something in the room moved on its own —
I swear I heard your voice inside the rain.
(inside the rain)

[Pre-Chorus 2]
The needle finds the groove and holds its breath.
(holds its breath)
Tomorrow I will drive to meet your stone.
(meet your stone)

[Chorus 2]
[piano enters softly, high register]
Heavy strings, heavy hands,
heavy years and how I grew.
(how I grew)
The chord I finally found
is the word I should have said to you.
(should have said)
I'll press until I finally make it through,
(make it through)
I'll press until it's TRUUUE.
(until it's true)

[Bridge]
[stripped back: guitar and voice only, almost spoken]
The cemetery gates don't open till eight.
(till eight)
I was there by seven, waiting at the road.
(at the road)
I wiped the stone and set the guitar down
on the leaves beside the gravel.
The wind came through the willow before I played one chord —
I heard you in it, just a little.
(just a little)
[strings swell slowly into the final chorus]

[Final Chorus]
[full strings, piano, choir harmonies, maximum emotional intensity]
Heavy strings, heavy hands —
I played it all the WAAAY.
(all the way)
I didn't miss a single string,
I heard you in the willow SWAAAY.
(in the willow sway)
The wire that once cut me
now sounds just like your NAAAME.
(sounds like your name)
Heavy strings, they're not so heavy now —
I finally CAAAME.
(finally came)

[Outro]
(Mmm-hmm...)
(Ohhh-ohhh...)
[cello solo]
[silence — CUT, do not fade]
